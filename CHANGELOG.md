## 0.3.0 — local accuracy repair

- Automatically recognize supported text pages by paragraphs and rows using only public Apple APIs.
- Corroborate observed OCR tokens across local passes; retain candidate evidence and an explicit whole-image mode.
- Protect text outside proposed crops; keep complex/unsupported layouts on the original path.
- Preserve legacy receipt meaning/rendering while recording schema-3 evidence.
- No new runtime dependencies, AI transcription, or publication.
